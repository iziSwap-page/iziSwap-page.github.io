iZiSwap Concentrated Liquidity Model

iZiSwap uses a concentrated liquidity AMM model that allows liquidity providers to allocate capital within specific price ranges, increasing capital efficiency and potentially generating higher fee returns compared to traditional constant-product pools that distribute liquidity uniformly.

Concentrated liquidity was popularized in 2021 to address capital inefficiency in early AMMs, enabling significantly more trading volume per dollar of locked value.

Capital efficiency determines how much volume a pool can handle relative to total value locked. By narrowing ranges, providers amplify exposure around active prices.

After comparing pool structures, we observed tighter spreads in active ranges on iZiSwap versus broad constant-product pools.

Custom Price Ranges

Liquidity providers select upper and lower bounds. Fees accrue only when trades occur within that band, encouraging active management rather than passive deposit strategies.

Capital Efficiency in Practice

Imagine renting out rooms only when demand peaks instead of leaving an entire building half-used. Concentration directs resources where activity happens most.

Risks of Active Management

If price exits the chosen range, assets stop earning fees and become fully exposed to one side of the pair, increasing directional risk.
