What is iZiSwap?

iZiSwap is iZUMi Finance's exchange product: a discretized-liquidity AMM for token swaps, liquidity positions, and limit-order style trading. The official product docs describe the DL-AMM model and app route.

How iZiSwap works

  1. Open the appGo to the official swap route and connect a wallet on a supported network.
  2. Choose tokensSelect the token you want to swap from and the token you want to receive.
  3. Review the quoteCheck the network, token addresses, estimated output, price impact, and approval request.
  4. Confirm in walletSign only on the verified domain and wait for the transaction to settle on-chain.

Before you swap

Built around DL-AMM

The design uses discrete price points for concentrated liquidity and supports limit-order style methods described in the developer docs.

Supported across networks

iZiSwap contracts are documented across BNB Chain and multiple EVM networks; switch only to networks shown in the live app.

iZiSwap FAQ

What is iziswap?

iziswap is iZUMi Finance's non-custodial DEX product for swapping tokens through a discretized-liquidity AMM model.

Is this iziswap page the official app?

No. This is a routing page; the official app is izumi.finance/trade/swap. Always verify the domain before connecting a wallet.

Which networks does iziswap support?

The deployed contracts docs list BNB Chain, zkSync Era, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, and other networks; live availability depends on the app route and token.

Can I place limit orders on iziswap?

The product docs describe limit-order style trading supported by the DL-AMM design; review live market conditions in the app before signing.

Do I need to trust this iziswap landing page?

Use it as a shortcut only; wallet connection, approvals, and swaps should happen on izumi.finance after you verify the URL.
