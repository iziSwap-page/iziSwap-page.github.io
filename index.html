What is iZiSwap

What iZiSwap is

A concentrated-liquidity DEX

iZiSwap, from the iZUMi project, is a decentralized exchange built on concentrated liquidity. Liquidity providers place funds inside chosen price ranges instead of across the whole curve, so more of the capital sits where trades actually happen.

An on-chain order-book model

The protocol also uses an order-book model, where liquidity can sit at specific price points, and supports limit-style orders alongside swaps. It is live on zkSync Era and other layer-2 networks, where gas is generally lower than on Ethereum mainnet.

Concentrated vs classic

Concentrated vs classic AMM pools

A classic pool spreads liquidity over every possible price. A concentrated pool lets providers choose where their liquidity works.

Classic AMM pool vs concentrated liquidity
Classic AMMConcentrated
Liquidityspread across all pricesplaced in chosen price ranges
Capital usemuch of it sits idlefocused where trades occur
Provider effortlow, set and forgethigher, ranges need monitoring
Provider riskimpermanent lossimpermanent loss, stronger if price leaves the range

In practice

For a trader, deeper liquidity near the current price can mean less price impact on a swap. For a provider, a narrow range can earn more fees per unit of capital but stops earning when the price leaves it.

How to swap

How to swap on iZiSwap

Route and quote

After you choose a pair and an amount, the interface finds a path through the available pools and shows the expected output, price impact and slippage tolerance.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and make sure it is on zkSync Era or the layer-2 you want.
  2. Select the token to sell and the token to buy, then enter an amount.
  3. Review the quote, the route, the pool fee tier and the estimated gas.
  4. Approve the sell token once if it is an ERC-20 you have not used here before.
  5. Sign the swap; it settles on-chain and the bought token appears in your wallet.

Native ETH does not need an approval, only the swap transaction.

Fees & gas

What a swap costs

Gas

On zkSync Era, gas is paid in ETH. Approving and swapping are separate transactions, each with its own gas cost. See how gas works.

Pool fee and price impact

Each pool charges a fee tier that goes to its liquidity providers. Together with price impact and your slippage tolerance, it is reflected in the quoted output.

Keep ETH for gas

Holding stablecoins or other tokens does not pay for gas. Keep a little ETH on the network you use, or you will not be able to sign.

Security

Staying safe

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. This interface quotes and routes; it never holds your funds or private keys.

Before you sign

  • Verify token contract addresses on the layer-2 you are using; lookalike tokens are common.
  • Approve only what you need and revoke old ERC-20 allowances.
  • Check price impact and slippage, especially in thin pools.
  • Liquidity providers face impermanent loss, and a concentrated range can stop earning if price leaves it.
  • Smart contracts can contain bugs, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

iZiSwap FAQ

What is iZiSwap?

iZiSwap is a decentralized exchange from the iZUMi project that combines concentrated liquidity with an on-chain order-book model. It runs on zkSync Era and other layer-2 networks.

What does concentrated liquidity mean?

Liquidity providers put their funds into a chosen price range rather than across every price. More capital sits near the current price, which can reduce price impact for traders.

What is the order-book model?

Instead of only a smooth price curve, liquidity can be placed at specific price points, which allows limit-style orders. Trades still settle on-chain from your own wallet.

Why trade on zkSync Era?

zkSync Era is a layer-2 rollup on Ethereum that uses validity proofs, and transactions there generally cost less gas than on mainnet. Gas is still paid in ETH, so keep a small balance.

Do I need to approve a token before swapping?

For ERC-20 tokens, yes. A one-time allowance lets the router move the token for you, and it is a separate transaction with its own gas. Native ETH needs no approval.

Does this interface hold my funds?

No. It is non-custodial: you connect your own wallet and sign each transaction, and tokens move directly between your wallet and on-chain contracts. There is no account to create.
