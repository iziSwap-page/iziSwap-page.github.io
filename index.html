Concentrated Liquidity on iziSwap: Range Basics (What You Must Understand)

Concentrated liquidity means LP capital is placed within a price range. If price moves outside your range, your position becomes less effective (and fee generation can drop).

  • In-range: your capital provides liquidity and earns fees.
  • Out-of-range: your position may stop earning (depends on design), and your asset composition shifts.
  • Range width: narrow ranges can earn more fees but require more active management.
LP reality: Range LP is a strategy, not passive income. If you won’t rebalance, choose wider ranges (or don’t LP).

Impermanent loss (IL) — practical view

  • IL is the “cost” of being the counterparty as price moves.
  • Fees can offset IL, but it depends on volume and your time in-range.
  • Track net outcome: fees earned − IL − gas − rebalancing cost.
Best LP habit: define your rebalance rule before you deposit (price move threshold + time schedule).

iziSwap Troubleshooting: Common Issues, Root Causes, Fixes

“Swap failed / reverted”

  • Slippage too tight and price moved.
  • Liquidity across the current range was insufficient.
  • Gas settings were too low.

“Token not showing in wallet”

  • Wrong chain selected.
  • Token not imported by contract address.
  • Confused token with a look-alike contract.

“LP position not earning”

  • Price moved out of your range.
  • Range too narrow for current volatility.
  • Fee tier / pair selection was mismatched to volume.
Best debugging method: verify state using on-chain explorers and dashboards first, UI second.
